The Elephant

My favorite animal is hands down an elephant ever since i came across a head face to face in Kariba. I love that they are the largest land animals and that they are known for their intelligence. They are also known for their strong family bonds, unity and also an impressive memory.

Facts about Elephants

  • Habitat: African savannas and Asian forests
  • Diet: Herbivore, eats grass, leaves, and bark
  • Lifespan: Up to 70 years in the wild
